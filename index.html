About Ghost

Every skid tells
a story.

Born and raised on the streets of Sosha, Ghost didn't learn to drive — he learned to perform. Behind the wheel of his legendary BMW 325i E30, known to the culture as the Gusheshe, he's turned spinning from a pastime into an art form.

What started at local gatherings has evolved into stadium appearances, brand partnerships, and music video features.

This isn't reckless. This is precision. This is years of understanding weight, momentum, and the thin line between chaos and control.
